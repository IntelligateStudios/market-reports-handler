## Decided without asking

- One agent writes `documents/background-recommendations.md` and `documents/background-data.json` from the sixteen finished art runs in Games-AI-art; the main session builds it into the summary as its own page, "Backgrounds" (`#backgrounds`), and links it from Next steps.
- "Slot background" is read as the reel window: what sits behind the symbols. "Game background" is the scene or field around it.
- Measures are taken from a real base-game screenshot of each game, region by region, and labelled measured; what a background is and how many elements it holds is read from the pictures and labelled read.
- "Theo ranking" is the Theo Win Index rank of each report's slot ranking; the agent states one sort rule across the five editions, as ours.
- It covers the sixteen analysed leaders; the leaders being analysed now join when the section is next written.
