> Three more reports (September, October and November 2026 editions) were handled after this was written. They add 27 leaders that are being analysed now. This text still describes the sixteen games analysed from the July and August editions and is written again when the new leaders are settled.

Written 27 Nov 2026. These are recommendations, not findings: each is our inference from the sixteen analysed leaders, and names the games it rests on. [Art of the leaders](#art-report) holds the evidence. They are written for the studio's AI art work (the Games AI art project: a reference game's art out, our own theme's art in, and the agentic test against what our artists delivered).

**Read this first.** The evidence covers IGT, Octoplay, Light & Wonder, AGS and Inspired. Fourteen of the 30 leaders, including all eight of Games Global's, could not be analysed. A recommendation here says "the leaders we could open do this", not "the market does this". The first step below is about closing that gap.

**See also** [Backgrounds](#backgrounds): recommendations on the game background, the reel window, the number of background elements, contrast and saturation, with the sixteen games measured and sorted by Theo rank (written 27 Nov 2026 at Roei's request).

## In short

1. Close the evidence gap before leaning on these conclusions: the Games Global games, and the bonus rounds we did not see.
2. Start every game's art from a written colour code and one emblem, before any picture is made.
3. Compose the base game around the prize, on a dark, plain reel window.
4. Plan the art package as stills plus a shared effects kit, and use generated art where the leaders' own art is stills.
5. Make feature modes and tiers by recolour, regrade and swap, and brief them as sets.
6. Treat a new theme on a proven frame as a normal way to make a game.
7. Take one or both of two looks into a concept: the classic machine re-dressed, and the glossy cartoon cast.
8. Build the tool pieces that limited this study, a Spine 4.x renderer first.

## 1. Close the evidence gap

- **Games Global (8 leaders).** Every public demo refused this container because its traffic leaves from the United States. Either run the capture from a studio machine in a country the demos serve, or ask the product team for demo links. This is the largest hole in the picture.
- **White Hat Studios (2 leaders).** No public demo. A sibling of each opens on Blueprint Gaming's demo server (Cash Strike Hotstepper 2, Cash Strike Power Force 5); that they are the same games under European names is inference. Decide whether they may stand in, labelled as stand-ins.
- **Aristocrat, Evolution, ReelPlay, IGT's Cash Eruption High Stakes (4 leaders).** Each needs a link or an account from the product team.
- **Bonus rounds not seen.** A played session of one to two hours per game, or the provider's own test tools where the studio has them, would turn "read from the files" into "seen" for [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel) and the three Huff N Puff games.

## 2. Begin with a colour code and one emblem

**Do:** make the first page of every art brief a table, written before any image is generated: one colour per prize tier, one per feature, one for "money won", and the surfaces each must appear on (symbol, frame, plaque, effect, win word, buy card). Add one emblem or mark and the list of pieces that carry it.

**Why:** this is the most consistent thing the analysed leaders do. Colour names the feature or the tier everywhere in [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption), [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win), [777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar) and all three Huff N Puff games. One emblem or mark ties plain objects into one game in [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor) (the gong), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel) (the snout) and [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win) (the clover).

**For generated art in particular:** a fixed code is what keeps separately generated pieces looking like one game. The 777 Hot Reels analysis names the hues that would have to be fixed first (#0b37b7 blue, #c366f7 purple, #c50b05 red, #f9c013 gold); our briefs should carry the same kind of line.

**In the agentic test:** add "colour code held" to what reviewers check. A generated package can score well piece by piece and still break the code.

## 3. Compose around the prize, on a dark plain board

**Do:** treat the base-game layout as a brief of its own, with three fixed decisions.

- **The reel window is dark and plain**, and every symbol has one colour of its own (a tile, a cloth, a halo or a rim). Rests on [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel) and [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win).
- **Warm symbols, cool ground** (or the reverse), decided on purpose. Rests on [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption) and [Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand).
- **The top prizes and the feature's progress are in the picture from the first spin**, as an object that changes: a wheel behind the reels, goals that fill, a statue that heats, a house front that is the prize board. Rests on the three Huff N Puff games, [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar) and [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption).

**Do not spend on scenery first.** None of the sixteen puts its budget into a landscape, and five have no scene at all ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win), [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)).

## 4. Stills plus a kit: where generated art fits

The analysts were asked to say, game by game, what an AI art pipeline could make. Their answers agree.

| Piece | Fit for generated art | Rests on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backgrounds, and a second scene by regrade | Good. This is where an image model gives most. | [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win), [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar) |
| Symbol stills (objects, fruit, still-life wares) | Good, when the colour code and finish are fixed first | [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win) |
| A character as one strong still, cut into a few parts | Reachable | [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win) |
| Character variants on one layout (three pigs, three snakes) | Reachable, as a consistency brief | [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win) |
| Glows, masks, dimmed and blurred copies | Derive from the stills; do not generate | [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel) |
| Short fire, burst and sparkle loops on black or in white | Possible; colour consistency across frames is the risk, which is why the leaders ship them white and tint | [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption), [Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win) |
| Frames, plates, lettering, ornament | Needs tight art direction: exact shape language and clean edges | [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar) |
| Acted sequences, destruction, wind | An animator's job | the three Huff N Puff games |
| 3D transitions (a vault door, turning gems) | A 3D render job | [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar) |

**Do:**

- **Define the static package as a list of stills**, per game, in the brief. The Huff N Puff analyses give the scale: one or two backgrounds, 16 to 18 symbol stills at about 300 px, a set of tiered objects with frames and backings, a coin, wedges and plates, a logo.
- **Build one studio effects kit in white**: sparkle, smoke, burst, lightning and flame loops, tinted per game. Half of [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)'s drawn frames and more than half of [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)'s are such effects, and [777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged) reuses another game's flame files.
- **Take "a still plus overlays" as the first motion target**, before rigs: reel picture, outline glow, spark and one loop, as in [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption). Two leaders ship no rig at all ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)).
- **Plan each symbol's outputs up front**: still, dimmed copy, blurred copy, additive light copy. Each of these appears in several leaders, and all can be derived from the still.
- **Cut for animation from the start.** A breakable symbol is one painting, 14 shards, two crack overlays and an "inside" picture ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)); a house exists whole and in pieces ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).
- **Keep words out of pictures.** Words separate from coins, plaques and splashes make localisation a handful of files (all six Octoplay games), and they also spare an image model from drawing letters.

## 5. Brief modes and tiers as sets

**Do:** ask for variants as a set in one brief, never one by one.

- **A feature mode** is the same composition recoloured or regraded: "day and dusk of one picture" ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win), [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)), or one hue flipped while the board stays ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged), [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)).
- **A tier** is the same object in another material at the same canvas size: straw, stick, brick; steel, diamond, gold; pink, gold ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand), [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)).
- **Neutral art tinted in the engine** where a mode only changes colour: a grey reel board ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win), [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).

**For the tools:** this is a natural next piece of the concept step. Given one approved still, produce its regrade, its material swaps and its dimmed, blurred and light copies at fixed sizes, and measure that each stays inside the colour code.

## 6. A new theme on a proven frame

**Do:** treat re-dressing a working game as a first-class way to make a new one, and say so in the plan.

**Why:** [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals) ranks among the leaders and, by its file names, is the building-site game with football art in every slot (inference). The six Octoplay leaders stand on one framework and share almost no picture. [777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged) is a "charged" version made from two logo words, 25 lightning frames and a jackpot row.

**This is what the studio's concept step already does**: it takes a reference game's extracted package and fills it piece for piece with our own theme's art. The leaders show the same method in production. The art package extracted from each analysed game is such a frame, with every piece named and sized.

**Mind the seam.** A shared feature left in another finish looks bolted on ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)'s wheel). When a piece is reused across our games, redraw its surface in the game's finish.

## 7. Two looks worth a concept each

These are suggestions for what to try, not predictions of what will sell. The market reports rank games; they do not say why a game ranks.

- **The classic machine, re-dressed.** Seven of the sixteen analysed leaders keep fruit, sevens, letters or money icons plain and put the art into one money object and one effect family. Their static art is small (a 3x3 or 5x3 set, one emblem, a plaque set), which suits a first fully generated package. References: [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win).
- **The glossy cartoon cast.** Six of the sixteen, five of them on pigs. It needs consistent characters and acted animation, so it tests the harder half of the pipeline. References: [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus) for a lean version, [Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand) for the full one.

## 8. Tool work this study asks for

In order of what it would unlock:

1. **A Spine 4.x renderer.** Twelve of the sixteen games use Spine 4.0 to 4.2 and none of their rigs could be drawn. Until then no skeleton frame of such a game is published.
2. **Readers for three provider formats**, now living as per-run scripts: Light & Wonder's sheets (three runs carry a copy of the same script), Octoplay's skeleton bundle and packed pages, Inspired's packed manifests. Each belongs in the extraction tool.
3. **A capture that waits for the game, not for a clock**, and a play driver that can scroll a paytable and hold a long session. Loading took from under a minute to 35 minutes across these games.
4. **Measures by art component**, not over the whole package, so one character's frames cannot swing a game's palette ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)).
5. **A build audit for our own packages**: duplicate sequences, near-identical rigs and stray files, the waste found in [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption) and [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel).
