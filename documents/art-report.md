Written 27 Nov 2026 from the sixteen finished art analyses. Every statement names the games it rests on; each game's own report holds the sheets, the real screens and the measured figures behind it.

## What this covers, and what it does not

The two market reports handled so far (Eilers-Fantini, U.S. Online Game Performance, July and August 2026 editions) name 30 leading slot games between them. **16 are analysed. 14 could not be**, and the gap is not random:

| Provider | Leaders | Analysed | Why the rest are missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IGT | 6 | 5 | Cash Eruption High Stakes has no public demo yet |
| Octoplay | 6 | 6 | |
| Light & Wonder | 3 | 3 | |
| AGS | 1 | 1 | |
| Inspired | 1 | 1 | |
| Games Global | 8 | 0 | Every public demo is refused for visitors from the United States, where our analysis machine sits |
| White Hat Studios | 2 | 0 | No public demo; the provider's own is behind a client login |
| Aristocrat | 1 | 0 | Demo only behind an account |
| Evolution | 1 | 0 | Access denied and an age gate |
| ReelPlay | 1 | 0 | The game server did not answer |

So this is the art of **IGT, Octoplay and Light & Wonder with two guests**, not of the whole list. Games Global, the largest single group among the leaders, is absent, and nothing below should be read as describing its games.

Three more limits apply to every game:

- **Bonus rounds are often read, not seen.** A free-play session reaches what chance gives it. Where a feature did not come, its art is read from the files and marked so in the game's report.
- **Most rigs are not drawn.** Our tool draws Spine 3.x skeletons. Twelve of the sixteen games use Spine 4.x, so their motion is read from the skeleton files, the frame sequences and the real screens.
- **Measured figures describe the art package, not the screen.** Brightness, warmth and palette are measured over every texture a game ships. A game with many frames of one character leans towards that character's colours ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion): three wolf rigs are 132 of its 349 textures).

## Three families of look

The sixteen games fall into three groups. The grouping is our reading.

| Family | Games | What defines it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The classic machine, re-dressed** | [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra), [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged), [Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win) | Fruit, sevens, bells, letters or money icons, kept plain. The art effort goes into the money object and the feature, not the paytable. Little or no scenery, and at most one character. |
| **The glossy cartoon cast** | [Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand), [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals), [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion), [Super Cash Boost Big Bad Bonus](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), [Bigger Piggy Bank Superwheel](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel), [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win) | Toy-bright, rounded, glossy characters and props in a storybook or stage setting, with the prizes built into the scene. |
| **The themed world with one figure** | [Mystery Of The Lamp Secret Bazaar](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win) | A place or a culture (bazaar, Chinese good fortune, lost temple) carried by one figure or one emblem, with realistic or ornamental objects as symbols. |

Two things stand out across the families:

- **Pigs.** Five of the sixteen games are built on pigs: the three Huff N Puff games and Super Cash Boost tell the Three Little Pigs, and Bigger Piggy Bank is a piggy-bank heist. A sixth leader, Coin Collect Piggy Patrons, carries pigs in its title; it is blocked, so what its art looks like is not known.
- **No game here is dark, gritty or realistic in its characters.** Where a figure appears it is friendly: a laughing emperor ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor)), a friendly genie ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)), a laughing leprechaun ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)), toy pigs, and a wolf dressed as a referee ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)).

## What the leaders share

### 1. Colour is a code, not decoration

Every analysed game that has prize tiers or several features gives each one a fixed colour and repeats it on every surface that names it. This is the single most consistent finding.

- **Per feature:** red, green and blue each mean one feature on fireball, border, volcano, label and buy card ([Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)); red Collect, green Clone, blue Double on head, gems, lights and words ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)); one hue per lamp ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)).
- **Per prize tier:** on coin, plaque, wheel wedge and win word ([Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win), [Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand), [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals), [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor)).
- **As the whole design:** blue is Cash, gold is Jackpot, red is both, down to the three words of the logo ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)); a blue 7, a purple 77 and a red 777 each tied to the jackpot of its colour ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)).
- **As a scale:** the same five rainbow steps rank the royals and the jackpots ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)); straw, wood and brick rank Wilds, houses, prize ladders and win tiers ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).

### 2. A dark, plain reel window, and warm symbols on a cool ground

The symbols are kept readable by what is behind them, not by outlines or size.

- A black or near-black board with saturated symbols: [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win).
- A cool cell under warm symbols: a teal board in a red room ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)); a background measured 84% cool behind feature symbols measured 88% warm ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand)).
- One colour per symbol, so the board is read by colour before shape: a cloth colour per symbol ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)), a tile colour per high symbol ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains)), a halo per symbol ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)).
- A photoreal backdrop can sit behind stylised symbols when the board between them is dark and plain ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)).

### 3. The prize is always in sight

The top prizes and the feature's progress are part of the base-game picture, not a meter in a corner.

- **The scene is the prize board:** the mansion's front holds the prizes ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)); fifteen prize plates take more of the screen than the reels ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)); the cabinet shows every pay above the reels ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)).
- **A prize wheel turns behind see-through reels** on every spin ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand), [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)).
- **Progress is an object that changes:** lamps with growing gold piles ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)); lava rising on three cones ([Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)); goals filling with balls ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)); a stone serpent heating to lava ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)); pots filling with gold ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)).
- **Jackpot plaques stand beside or above the reels** through the base game: six down the side ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand)), four at the side ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)), five banners above ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)), three ladders around the reels ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).

### 4. Scenery is cheap or absent

None of the sixteen spends its budget on a landscape.

- **No scene at all:** a flat green field ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains)); a patterned wall ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor)); a one-colour dusk ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)); tinted white light shapes and not one background picture ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)); a shamrock pattern ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)).
- **One picture, unlayered:** single JPEG backgrounds ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor)).
- **Depth from light, not painting:** mirrored ray pictures and eight views of one 3D sign ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)).
- The cartoon family paints more (a storybook garden, a stadium, a mansion, a valley), and still as one picture per scene.

### 5. One emblem, motif or figure carries the game

- **One emblem at several sizes:** the gong is symbol, title coin, trigger and bonus wheel centre ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor)).
- **One creature for every mechanic:** the snake is Wild, feature symbols, collectors, progress statue and screen wipe ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)).
- **One small mark on plain objects:** a pig's snout on at least nine pieces ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)); a snout on each letter and tool ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)); a heart-leaf clover on coin, pipe, hat and Scatter ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)); a step-fret on every frame and button ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption)).
- **One drawing used several ways:** one face as loading picture, logo and wild ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)); one flaming seven recoloured as the whole high-symbol set, the Wild and the logo ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)); one leprechaun rig as intro figure and two symbols ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)); one train picture ([Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win)).
- **One effect family:** blue lightning ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains)), white lightning ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)), tinted smoke ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)), flat white smoke and wind ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).

### 6. A feature mode is a recolour, a regrade or a re-dress

- **Recolour only:** the same background in red with a red-edged reel window ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains)); backdrop hue and cell ground ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)); the same hall pushed to red ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption)); purple night to orange blaze ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)); tint values alone ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)); green to purple ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)).
- **Regrade of one picture:** day jungle to dusk ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)); day and night stadium on one drawing ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)); day and sunset on one composition ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).
- **The exceptions repaint:** a separate crater scene for the bonus ([Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)), a cave beside the bazaar ([Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar)), a night mansion with its own lit windows ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)).

### 7. Grades and tiers are swaps on one rig

- Three saws, four houses and eleven banners on shared rigs and canvas sizes ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand)); two ball colours, two goals and three trophy metals ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)); straw, stick and brick ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)).
- Three Snakes on one rig ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)); three pigs on one portrait layout ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).
- Pink to gold as the upgrade for every pig, at four sizes ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)).
- One cone in three colours and one fireball in four ([Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)).

### 8. The celebration is made from the cast

- The big win is the wolf blowing down a better house per tier, with the game's own coin, hat and saw as particles ([Huff N Even More Puff Grand](#game-lnw-huff-n-even-more-puff-grand), read from the files); the wolf and three trophies ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals), read from the files).
- The BIG WIN reuses the Wild pig's bust and the scene's props ([Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus), seen).
- Small wins are quiet: ten times the bet got a gold burst and a caption ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals), seen).

## How they are built

### Stills first, motion on top

| How motion is made | Games |
| --- | --- |
| Frame sequences and stills only, no rig | [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains) (854 frames), [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra) (611 frames) |
| Timelines over still pictures, with short fire loops | [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption) |
| Spine rigs moving still pictures, plus drawn sequences for fire, smoke and light | [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), all six Octoplay games |
| Spine rigs, with whole drawn sequences for acting and destruction | the three Huff N Puff games |
| Spine rigs of small parts on almost every symbol | [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel) (64 skeletons) |

The pattern under the table: **a strong still per piece, and a shared kit of overlays.** A win version is the reel picture plus an outline glow, a spark and a flame loop ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption)). A full-height character feels alive with four moving pieces and two flame loops over a painting ([Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption)). A train is one picture with animated wheel, rods and sparks ([Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win)). A statue is one still with glow layers ([Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)).

Where real animation is spent, it is spent in one place: the wolf's face and the big-win pigs ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)), a 25-frame turn for each diamond ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)), an 80-frame wild ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)), a pig smashing into 14 pre-cut shards ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)). The Light & Wonder games state the rule: rig what moves mechanically, draw what is chaotic.

### Production habits worth knowing

- **Effects ship white and are tinted in the game.** One flame sequence serves every jackpot colour ([Fire Rail Express](#game-octoplay-fire-rail-express-hold-and-win)); the same flame files serve two games ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)); half of all drawn frames are reusable white sparkle and smoke ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)); also [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win) and [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win).
- **Light effects are drawn on black and added**, apart from the solid art ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)); an additive "Light" copy of each symbol makes its win flash ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)).
- **Boards ship grey and are tinted per mode** ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win), [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus)).
- **Words are separate from what they label** and sit in a few localised files (all six Octoplay games; English and Spanish versions in the Huff N Puff games; six languages of win words in [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)).
- **Each reel symbol ships sharp and motion-blurred** ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Mystery Of The Lamp](#game-igt-mystery-of-the-lamp-secret-bazaar), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)).
- **Numbers are treated as art:** several drawn numeral sets, each tied to one place ([Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), [Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption), [Serpent Gold](#game-octoplay-serpent-gold-hold-and-win)); values baked into pictures ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains)).
- **The loading screen sells the features** as three captioned cards ([Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)).

### New games on proven frames

- **A reskin, piece for piece.** [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals) keeps the building-site games' file and animation names (saw, hard hat, houses, toolboxes) under football art. That it is the earlier game re-dressed is inference from those names; the mechanics, rigs and timings look kept and only the pictures changed.
- **One framework, six looks.** The six Octoplay games share a shell, fonts, a file layout and many skeleton names, and still share almost no picture of their own art. The same framework carries a neon scoreboard with no scene ([Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win)) and an illustrated character game ([The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win)).
- **A sequel from little new art:** two extra logo words, 25 lightning frames and a jackpot row ([777 Hot Reels Megacharged](#game-octoplay-777-hot-reels-megacharged)).

### What it costs when it goes wrong

- Three near-identical wolf rigs on 132 atlas pages and a stray sequence from another game ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)).
- The same sequences packed several times ([Triple Cash Eruption](#game-igt-triple-cash-eruption)); five 4096 px pages for one door opening ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)); 22 pages for one dust blast ([Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)).
- A shared platform feature left in another finish looks bolted on ([Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel)'s Super Wheel).

## The measured picture

The tables under this text hold each game's palette, tone and package size, all measured from pixels. Read across them:

- **Dark packages.** Twelve of the sixteen measure under 0.5 in brightness; the four above are [Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals) (0.65), [Mega Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-mega-cash-boost-hold-and-win) (0.63), [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion) (0.55) and [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus) (0.51).
- **Warm packages.** Thirteen measure warmer than cool. The exceptions are [Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion) (cool 0.64, pulled by its wolf frames), [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus) and [Capital Gains](#game-ags-capital-gains), both near balance. The warmest are [Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra) (0.90) and [Cash Eruption](#game-igt-cash-eruption) (0.87).
- **A very dark colour leads most palettes.** The largest palette colour is black or a near-black tint in eleven of the sixteen games.
- **Size says little about rank.** Packages run from 19 textures and 705 sprites ([Cleopatra](#game-igt-cleopatra)) to 349 textures and 3,579 sprites ([Huff N Puff Money Mansion](#game-lnw-huff-n-puff-money-mansion)). The Octoplay games hold 454 to 802 sprites each on two or three atlas pages.

## What is still open

- **Fourteen leaders, eight of them Games Global's.** They need a machine outside the United States, or demo links from the providers or the product team.
- **Bonus rounds not seen** in several games: the Hold & Win of [Super Cash Boost](#game-octoplay-super-cash-boost-big-bad-bonus) and [The Lucky Fella](#game-octoplay-the-lucky-fella-hold-and-win), every feature of the three Huff N Puff games, the feature scenes of [Money Gong Emperor](#game-igt-money-gong-emperor), the Super Wheel of [Bigger Piggy Bank](#game-inspired-bigger-piggy-bank-superwheel). Longer played sessions would settle them.
- **Rigs not drawn** for the twelve Spine 4.x games.
- **Desktop landscape only.** Portrait and phone layouts were not captured for any game; several ship them.
- **[Huff N Lots Of Goals](#game-lnw-huff-n-lots-of-goals)** was read from the UK build; that its art matches the US game is inference.
