Glossary
Devices (Desktop/Mobile/Tablet):
Device level performance and metrics are directly reported from each individual operator. While the majority of Operator provide this level there are some who elect to roll
their databases into aggregated performance at the Theme level. Mobile is inclusive of Phones while Tablet is segmented separately.

Operating System:
Where the data set is available, we also ask supplier to provide performance by operating system segmenting iOS devices (iPhones & iPads) from Android based phones and
tablets.

NEW Game Themes:
The classification of NEW consists of all game themes that are new to the OGPD in the past 180 days, we tag each game with a timestamp as it enters our database.

Fair Share:
Comparing the percent of total Themes from a Supplier to the percent of Total Theo Win generated fair share is calculation representing if a supplier is generating the Theo to
justify the number of Themes.
